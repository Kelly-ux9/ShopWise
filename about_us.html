ABOUT SHOPWISE

Connecting African products with global opportunity.

ShopWise is a digital marketplace concept built from Rwanda to make African products easier to discover and trade across borders. The platform gives sellers a professional storefront while helping buyers discover products and their origins.

What ShopWise enables

  • Product discovery: browse products by category and search.
  • Seller storefronts: publish product information, pricing and origin.
  • Digital orders: authenticated buyers can place orders linked to the selected product.

Our vision

We envision a stronger digital bridge between African entrepreneurs and customers around the world—starting with practical tools that make local products visible, understandable and easier to trade.
